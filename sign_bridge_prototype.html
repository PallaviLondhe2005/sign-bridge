Sign Bridge

Multi-language sign language learning & converter app: working prototype (SIH PS2)

Speech / Text → Sign

Tap Convert to see the sign sequence.

Solid cards are whole signs. Dashed cards are letters: words without a sign are spelled out letter by letter. Sign animation clips are placeholders in this prototype (cards stand in for the clips).

Sign → Speech (simulation)

The real camera recognition (MediaPipe hand points + Random Forest model) runs in the Python prototype. Here you can try the app’s decision rule and speech output: pick a sign and set how sure the model is.
Pick a sign, then tap Recognise.

Rule used by the app: a sign is spoken only when confidence is at least 70%. Otherwise it asks the user to try again.

The 100% accuracy is on synthetic (generated) hand data. It shows the training pipeline works; it is not accuracy on real signs. Real accuracy will be measured after collecting webcam samples.
